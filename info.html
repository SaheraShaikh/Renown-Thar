About the Legendary Mahindra Thar

The Mahindra Thar is more than just an SUV — it is an icon of freedom, adventure, and rugged strength. Whether you're driving through bustling city streets or conquering the wildest terrains, the Thar is built to deliver power, reliability, and thrill.

Engines & Performance

The Thar is powered by two robust engine options:

  • 2.0L mStallion Turbo-Petrol – delivering up to 150 horsepower.
  • 2.2L mHawk Diesel – producing 130 horsepower with excellent fuel efficiency.

Both engines are available with manual and automatic transmissions, and the 4x4 drivetrain with high & low range ensures you’re always ready for any adventure.

Design & Comfort

The Thar is available in hard-top, soft-top, and convertible roof options, giving you the freedom to choose your adventure style. Inside, you’ll find premium seating for four passengers, smart storage spaces, and a modern design that balances comfort with ruggedness.

Technology & Features

  • 7-inch touchscreen infotainment system with Android Auto & Apple CarPlay.
  • Adventure stats & navigation for off-road tracking.
  • Water-resistant interiors designed for durability.

Safety First

Adventure doesn’t mean compromising on safety. The Thar is equipped with:

  • Dual airbags for driver and passenger.
  • ABS with EBD for improved braking.
  • Electronic Stability Program (ESP) with roll-over mitigation.
  • Hill-hold and hill-descent control for safe off-roading.

Why Thar?

From its rugged design to its off-road mastery, the Mahindra Thar isn’t just a car — it’s a lifestyle. It represents freedom, adventure, and the spirit of exploration, making it a favorite among SUV enthusiasts and adventurers across India.
