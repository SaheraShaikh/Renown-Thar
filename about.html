About the Mahindra Thar

The Mahindra Thar isn’t just a car – it’s a legend on wheels. Inspired by India’s iconic military Jeeps, the Thar first debuted in 2010 and immediately became a favorite among adventure lovers and off-road enthusiasts.

The Legacy & Evolution

Over the years, the Thar has evolved into a perfect blend of rugged toughness and modern comfort. Each new generation brings advanced technology, enhanced safety features, and improved performance, all while staying true to the iconic off-road DNA that makes it instantly recognizable.

Why the Thar is Special

The Thar is built for those who seek adventure. From rough off-road trails to smooth city roads, it delivers power, style, and reliability. Key features include:

  • 4x4 drivetrain with high & low range for ultimate off-road control.
  • Convertible, soft-top, and hard-top roof options for flexibility.
  • Bold, rugged design that turns heads everywhere.
  • Comfortable seating and modern interiors for long journeys.

Milestones & Achievements

  • Winner of the 2021 Indian Car of the Year (ICOTY).
  • One of the fastest-selling SUVs in India, often with long waiting lists after launch.
  • Favored by celebrities, off-road clubs, and adventure travelers.
  • Recognized for exceptional off-road performance in multiple terrain challenges.

The Spirit of Thar
